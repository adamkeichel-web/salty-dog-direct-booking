Clear expectations

Guest and booking policies.

Review the complete price, payment schedule, cancellation terms, and property rules before reserving.

Direct reservations are confirmed after the required payment succeeds through Stripe. Airbnb reservations remain governed by the terms shown during Airbnb checkout.

Reservations

A direct reservation is confirmed after secure checkout succeeds. Availability is checked again immediately before checkout begins.

Payments

Eligible stays may be paid in full or with 50% due at booking and the balance due 14 days before check-in. The exact amounts and dates are displayed before checkout.

Scheduled balance authorization

When a guest selects the split-payment option, the guest authorizes Salty Dog & Co. to automatically charge the saved payment method for the displayed balance on the displayed due date. Additional authentication or a new payment method may be required if the charge cannot be completed.

Cancellations

Cancellation and refund eligibility may vary by property and booking. Confirm the applicable terms before paying or contact us with questions.

House rules

Occupancy, pets, parking, quiet hours, smoking, events, pool use, and other rules vary by property and must be followed by all guests.

Privacy and security

Contact and reservation information is used to manage the stay. Payment details are entered with Stripe and are not collected by email or text.
