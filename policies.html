Clear expectations

Guest and booking policies.

The final terms for each reservation will be displayed before checkout and included in the rental agreement.

Draft policy framework: cancellation deadlines, payment schedules, deposits, pet fees, minimum age, and property-specific house rules must be finalized before direct booking is enabled.

Reservations

A reservation is confirmed only after availability is verified, required payment is accepted, and the rental agreement is completed.

Payments

Direct payments will be processed securely through Stripe. Final deposit and balance schedules will be presented before checkout.

Cancellations

The applicable cancellation and refund terms will appear with the selected property, dates, and rate before payment.

House rules

Occupancy, pets, parking, quiet hours, smoking, events, pool use, and other rules may vary by property and will appear on its page and agreement.

Privacy

Guest information will be used to answer inquiries, manage reservations, process payments, and provide stay-related support. Payment-card details will be handled by Stripe.

Third-party platforms

Reservations made through Airbnb or another marketplace remain governed by that platform’s checkout terms and the property rules shown there.
