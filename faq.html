Guest help

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to compare properties, plan your trip, and understand the direct-booking process.

Booking and availability

Can I book directly today?

Direct reservations will open after Beds24 availability and Stripe payments are connected and fully tested. Until then, use the Airbnb button on the relevant property page for current availability.

Will the website calendar match Airbnb?

Yes. Beds24 will become the central calendar and synchronize connected booking channels. We will test every listing before activating direct reservations.

Will direct prices match Airbnb?

Not necessarily. Direct rates, taxes, cleaning fees, deposits, and promotions will be shown clearly before checkout. Marketplace service fees may differ from direct-booking costs.

When is a reservation confirmed?

A direct reservation will be confirmed only after availability is verified, the required payment is accepted, and any required rental agreement is completed.

Choosing a property

Where are the properties?

The collection includes homes and condos in Navarre, Navarre Beach, and Fort Walton Beach, with access to Okaloosa Island, Destin, and the surrounding Emerald Coast.

Which properties allow pets?

The Salty Dog and Stars & Sea are currently identified as pet-friendly. Pet limits, approval requirements, and applicable fees will be shown with each property.

Which properties have pools?

Beachfront Bliss, Deep Blue Dive, Sea Turtle, Seaside Vibes, Stars & Sea, and Waterfront Paradise currently feature private or shared pool access.

How many guests can the homes accommodate?

Capacities range from four guests in the waterfront condos to eighteen guests at Deep Blue Dive. Use the guest selector on the stays page to filter the collection.

Payments and policies

How will direct payments work?

Stripe will securely process direct-booking payments. Payment-card details will not be stored in the website files.

What is the cancellation policy?

The applicable cancellation deadline and refund terms will be displayed before payment and included in the rental agreement. Platform reservations remain subject to the terms shown on that platform.

Are parties or events allowed?

Occupancy, event, quiet-hour, parking, smoking, pet, and pool rules vary by property. The final property rules will be presented before checkout.

When will I receive the address and entry instructions?

Confirmed guests will receive the exact address, arrival details, and access instructions according to the communication schedule for their reservation.
