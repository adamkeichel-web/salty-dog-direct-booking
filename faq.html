Frequently asked questions.

Helpful answers for choosing a property and planning an Emerald Coast stay.

Can I book directly today?

The site is currently in staging. Live direct reservations will open after Beds24 availability and Stripe payments are connected and tested.

Where are the properties located?

The collection includes stays in Navarre, Navarre Beach, and Fort Walton Beach, with convenient access to Okaloosa Island and Destin.

Which properties allow pets?

The Salty Dog and Stars & Sea are currently identified as pet-friendly. Always review the property-specific rules before booking.

Which properties have pools?

Beachfront Bliss, Deep Blue Dive, Sea Turtle, Seaside Vibes, Stars & Sea, and Waterfront Paradise feature private or shared pool access.

Will rates match Airbnb?

Exact pricing will come from Beds24 after integration. Direct prices, taxes, fees, minimum stays, and promotions will be displayed before checkout.

How will payments work?

Stripe will securely process direct-booking payments. Card information will not be stored in this website’s files.

What is the cancellation policy?

The final cancellation terms will be shown clearly before payment and included in the rental agreement.
