Welcome

Hi, I’m Taryn.

I founded Salty Dog & Co. to give guests a more personal way to experience Florida’s Emerald Coast. Every vacation home featured here is personally overseen, and I work directly alongside each homeowner.

My goal is simple: make sure every guest walks into a clean, comfortable home and has someone responsive to turn to throughout their stay. When you book with us, you’re working with someone who knows every home personally.

The story behind the name

Meet Ruby, our original salty dog.

Ruby’s painted portrait is at the heart of our brand—a reminder that the best places to stay should feel welcoming, familiar, and full of personality.

We partner closely with homeowners to offer a carefully selected collection rather than an endless catalog. That lets us provide the attentive communication and local knowledge that are often missing from larger booking companies.

Whether you’re planning a weekend getaway or a family beach vacation, we’re here to help you feel at home while creating memories that last long after your trip ends.
