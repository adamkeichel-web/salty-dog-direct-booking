Booking and availability

How do I check availability and book?

Choose a property, enter your dates and guest count, then select “Check dates on Airbnb.” Airbnb will show current availability, pricing, rules, and secure checkout.

Does this website show live availability?

The property pages send your selected dates and guest count to the matching Airbnb listing. Airbnb remains the source for current availability and pricing.

Can someone help me choose the right property?

Yes. Email taryn.sanford@yahoo.com or call/text 850-910-5390 for property-specific help before booking.

When is a reservation confirmed?

A reservation is confirmed according to the confirmation shown by Airbnb after its checkout requirements are completed.

Choosing a property

Where are the properties?

The collection includes homes and condos in Navarre, Navarre Beach, and Fort Walton Beach, with access to Okaloosa Island, Destin, and the surrounding Emerald Coast.

Which properties allow pets?

The Salty Dog and Stars & Sea are currently identified as pet-friendly. Confirm pet limits, approval requirements, and applicable fees on Airbnb before booking.

Which properties have pools?

Beachfront Bliss, Deep Blue Dive, Sea Turtle, Seaside Vibes, Stars & Sea, and Waterfront Paradise currently feature private or shared pool access.

How many guests can the homes accommodate?

Capacities range from four guests in the waterfront condos to eighteen guests at Deep Blue Dive. Use the guest selector on the stays page to filter the collection.

Payments and policies

How are payments handled?

Payments are completed securely through Airbnb. Never send payment-card information through the website inquiry form, email, or text.

What is the cancellation policy?

The applicable cancellation deadline and refund terms appear on the selected property’s Airbnb listing before payment.

Are parties or events allowed?

Occupancy, event, quiet-hour, parking, smoking, pet, and pool rules vary by property. Review the current listing rules before booking.

When will I receive the address and entry instructions?

Confirmed guests receive the exact address, arrival details, and access instructions according to Airbnb and the host’s communication schedule.
